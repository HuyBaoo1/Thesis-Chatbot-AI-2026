1.2. Requirement on Grade-Point-Average (GPA): a minimum GPA of 6.5 (10-point grading scale) or equivalent.

2\. Admission Requirements

2.1. Satisfied one of the following requirements for entrance English proficiency:

- Have a valid international English certificate equivalent to at least IELTS Academic 5.0 or TOEFL iBT 42 or equivalent at the application time. For GFE, applicants have IELTS Academic 5.5 or equivalent; or
- Pass VGU English test; or
- From a country where English is an official language (list of the related countries is provided by VGU [[linked annex]](https://vgu.edu.vn/documents/14921/6989713/Annex+2.pdf/39e98ea6-f6b9-4aec-bb8c-5d25a6acf0f0)); or
- Graduated from a bachelor or master program fully taught and assessed in English (offcial evidence required).

2.2. The total score of the entrance examination (10-point grading scale) is calculated from the weighted average of three components with the following ratio:

\- 25% of the Bachelor GPA (at least 6.5/10 point) ( _Table of GPA conversion between different grading systems [[linked annex]](https://vgu.edu.vn/documents/14921/6989713/Annex+3.pdf/5b6ea74b-e4a2-420a-a0d4-ff4114ec124b)_)

\- 50% of the technical Interview (at least 5.0/10 point)

\- 25% of the English score ( _Table of English proficiency tests equivalency [[linked annex]](https://vgu.edu.vn/documents/14921/10674296/Table+of+English+proficiency+tests+equivalency.pdf/62ab4458-9144-4852-89d8-dfa780165fc4)_)

**Notes:**

\- The **conditional admission letter** is offered to applicants who do not yet have IELTS Academic IELTS 6.0 or TOEFL iBT 60 or equivalent and those who have not submitted their bachelor's degree yet. VGU will organize Pre-Master Course(s) to enhance the English skills for those who cannot submit a proof of English language proficiency with a minimum band score of Academic IELTS 6.0 or TOEFL iBT 60 at the time of admission. Participants are responsible for paying the course fee regulated by VGU. Upon the completion of the Pre-Master Course(s), the participants are refunded 100% and 50% of the course fee if they submit IELTS Academic certificate with band score 7.0 and 6.5-6.0 respectively within 4 weeks. Further details on the Pre-Master Course(s) will be sent to the applicants upon the availability of their admission letters.

\- To be eligible for master thesis registration, students are required to provide a valid English certificate with either at least IELTS Academic 6.0 / TOEFL iBT 60 or the completion of an international recognized English exam, with a result equivalent to IELTS Academic 6.0.

\- Application is also allowed if the bachelor's degree is not yet available. To be invited to the entrance examination, applicants must submit the temporary statement of academic completion. VGU's Bachelor students, whose thesis is in progress, are allowed to apply. If admitted and enrolled, applicants must hand in the temporary statement of academic completion before 31 March 2027 and submit the bachelor's degree by 01 September 2027. Failure to meet these requirements will result in study expulsion, and tuition fees will not be reimbursed.
