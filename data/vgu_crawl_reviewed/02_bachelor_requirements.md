**Eligible applicants:**

**Eligible applicants must satisfy the following conditions:**

\- Applicants who have graduated from high school, vocational high school, or who are currently in Grade 12 at high school or vocational high school in Viet Nam or in foreign countries, recognized as equivalent to Vietnam's high school or vocational secondary school level by competent authorities;

\- Applicants who already hold a vocational college diploma in a discipline within the same field as the program they are applying for at VGU, and have completed the required knowledge at the high school level in accordance with the provisions of law;

\- Applicants who meet the admission requirements of VGU and the Ministry of Education and Training (MoET);

\- If applicants do not meet the above conditions, or have graduated from special high schools, VGU shall establish an academic committee to assess their competence as equivalent to the high school level.

**Requirements of each modes:**

**Mode 1 - TestAS**

**Mode 1 - TestAS**

**1\. Admission procedures:**

Applicants are required to complete the application form and submit the following mandatory documents (scanned copy) through VGU's online application system (https://apply.vgu.edu.vn):

\- Personal identity card/ Passport;

\- The High School graduation diploma/certificate (only required for applicants who have already graduated from high school);

\- Valid English certificate (if any)

Applicants with valid application dossiers will receive a notification to pay the entrance examination fees, which include the TestAS examination fee and the VGU English Test (VGU ETEST) fee (if applicable).

The TestAS examination fee is **VND 3,000,000 per applicant per test session**. If the applicant is required to take the VGU English Test (VGU ETEST), the examination fee is **VND 1,000,000 per applicant per test session**. All paid examination fees are **non-refundable under any circumstances**.

Applicants must complete the payment of the examination fees within the specified deadline in order to be invited to participate in the entrance examination. Upon confirmation of payment, applicants will receive an **the invitation letter**, which includes detailed information on the examination date, time, venue, permitted materials and supporting devices, as well as other relevant instructions.

On the examination day, applicants are required to bring the following documents:

- A valid **Citizen Identification Card or Passport**;
- The **The invitation letter issued by VGU** (hard copy).

2\. Entrance Examination subjects and total score

The computer-based test (Digital TestAS) consists of two components:

a **Core Module** and a **Subject Module**. The Subject Module is specified as follows:

- For CSE: Mathematics, Computer Science and Natural Sciences module;
- For ECE, MEN, SME, SPE, BCE, ARC, MEC, BME: Engineering module;
- For BFA, BBA, BSE, BDE: Economics module.
- The subject module is only valid for admission to the corresponding program(s);
- The total score of the Digital TestAS is calculated from the two component tests in the following ratio: 40% of the Core Module and 60% of the Subject Module.

For more information about the Sample test please click here [[linked annex]](https://vgu.edu.vn/en/admission/bachelor/sample-test)

**3\. Requirements for admission:**

Applicants will be admitted if they meet all of the following requirements:

- Satisfy English requirements.
- No component tests (Core Module and Subject Module) scores below 50 points
according to TestAS grading scale.
- Applicants must obtain a minimum total score of 15.00 out of 30 for three subjects (including compulsory Mathematics and Literature) in the High School Graduation Examination (or the Vocational High School Examination for applicants from Vocational Education), applicable to applicants graduating from 2026 onwards. This provision shall not apply to applicants who are granted special consideration for high school graduation, and to those who have graduated from international high school programs.
- Total score of the VGU entrance examination or total score of a valid TestAS certificate must be greater than or equal to VGU Admission scores for each study program which are decided by the Examination Committee based on the number of applicants that fulfill the conditions mentioned in above point of this clause and the admission quota. The admission scores are greater than or equal to minimum threshold point as stipulated in the Circular No. 06/2026/TT-BGDĐT dated 15 February 2026 issued by the Ministry of Education and Training..

Applicants are be ranked based on the their total scores of the entrance examination, from highest to lowest.If the number of qualified applicants is greater than the admission quota, and the last ranked applicants have equal total scores, those with higher English score will be given the priority for admission. The equivalency conversion among different English test formats shall be implemented in accordance with VGU regulations. If their English scores are also equal, then the applicants with higher subject module score will be given the priority for admission. The official admission list with ranking will be approved by the Examination Committee.

**Mode 2 - Assessment of high school academic results**

**Mode 2 - Assessment of high school academic results**

**1\. Eligible applicants:**

Only applicable for applicants who will graduate from high schools in Vietnam in the admission year

2\. Admission procedures:

Applicants are required to complete the application form and submit the following mandatory documents (scanned copy) through VGU's online application system (https://apply.vgu.edu.vn/en);

- Personal identity card/ passport;
- Authorized official High school academic records of three grades(Grades 10, 11 and 12) verified by the relevant high school;
- Valid English certificate (if any);

**3\. Requirements for selection:**

\- Based on the high school academic results of three grades (Grades 10, 11 and 12), the total average score (rounded to two decimal places) is calculated as the mean of the following subjects:

- **For BCE, ARC, MEN, ECE, MEC, SME:** six subjects, including Mathematics, Physics, Literature and three elective subjects. Three elective subjects can be chosen among Foreign language, Chemistry, Biology, Informatics, Technology, History and Geography.

**Total score** = (M x 25%) + (P x 15%) + (L x 15%) + (E1 x 15%) + (E2 x 15%) + (E3 x 15%)

In which, M, P, L, E1, E2, E3 are means of the respective subjects in Grade 10, Grade 11 and Grade 12 (i.e. M=(M10 + M11 + M12) ⁄ 3; L=(L10 + L11 + L12) / 3…)

- **For BME:** six subjects, including Mathematics, Physics, Literature and three elective subjects. Three elective subjects can be chosen among Foreign Language, Chemistry, Biology, Informatics, Technology, History and Geography; or six subjects, including Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry and three elective subjects. Three elective subjects can be chosen among Foreign Language, Literature, Biology, Informatics, Technology, History and Geography.

**Total score** = (M x 25%) + (P x 15%) + (L x 15%) + (E1 x 15%) + (E2 x 15%) + (E3 x 15%);

or

**Total score** = (M x 35%) + (P x 13%) + (C x 13%) + (E1 x 13%) + (E2 x 13%) + (E3x 13%);

In which, M, C, P, L, E1, E2, E3 are means of the respective subjects in Grade 10, Grade 11 and Grade 12 (i.e. M=(M10 + M11 + M12) ⁄ 3; L=(L10 + L11 + L12) ⁄ 3…)

- **For SPE:** six subjects, including Mathematics, Chemistry, Literature and three elective subjects. Three elective subjects can be chosen among Foreign language, Physics, Biology, Informatics, Technology, History and Geography; or six subjects, including Mathematics, Physics, Literature and three elective subjects. Three elective subjects can be chosen among Foreign language, Chemistry, Biology, Informatics, Technology, History and Geography.

**Total score** = (M x 25%) + (C x 15%) + (L x 15%) + (E1 x 15%) + (E2 x 15%) + (E3 x 15%);

or

**Total score**(M x 25%) + (P x 15%) + (L x 15%) + (E1 x 15%) + (E2 x 15%) + (E3 x 15%)

In which, M, C, P, L, E1, E2, E3 are means of the respective subjects in Grade 10, Grade 11 and Grade 12 (i.e. M=(M10 + M11 + M12) ⁄ 3; L=(L10 + L11 + L12) ⁄ 3…)

- **For CSE:** six subjects, including Mathematics, Informatics, Literature and three elective subjects. Three elective subjects can be chosen among Foreign language, Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Technology, History and Geography; or six subjects, including Mathematics, Physics, Literature and three elective subjects. Three elective subjects can be chosen among Foreign language, Informatics, Chemistry, Biology, Technology, History and Geography.

**Total score** = (M x 25%) + (IT x 15%) + (L x 15%) + (E1 x 15%) + (E2 x 15%) + (E3 x 15%);

or

**Total score** = (M x 25%) + (P x 15%) + (L x 15%) + (E1 x 15%) + (E2 x 15%) + (E3 x 15%)

In which, M, IT, P, L, E1, E2, E3 are means of the respective subjects in Grade 10, Grade 11 and Grade 12 (i.e. M=(M10 + M11 + M12 )⁄3; L=(L10 + L11 + L12 ) ⁄ 3...).

- **For BFA, BBA, BSE, BDE:** six subjects, including Mathematics, Literature, and four elective subjects. Four elective subjects can be chosen among Foreign language, Physics, Chemistry, Informatics, Technology, Economic and Legal Education, History and Geography.

**Total score** = (M x 25%) + (L x 15%) + (E1 x 15%) + (E2 x 15%) + (E3 x 15%) + (E4 x 15%)

In which, M, L, E1, E2, E3, E4 are means of the respective subjects in Grade 10, Grade 11 and Grade 12 (i.e. M=(M10 + M11 + M12)⁄3; L=(L10 + L11 + L12 ) ⁄ 3...).

\- Additional points: For each of the following achievements, applicants will have 0.25 points added to
the total score (maximum possible bonus points is 0.5 and the total score is not exceeded
10 points):

- Having a valid English certificate at least equivalent to IELTS Academic 6.0;
- Being currently a student of a gifted high school (Click here to see the list of gifted high schools). [[linked annex]](https://vgu.edu.vn/documents/14921/8572077/Annex+4.pdf/a155f525-4f81-45b5-8a25-e8a4b29ab5c3)

\- Applicants must satisfy the following requirements to be eligible for the selection

- The average grade in Mathematics, calculated as the arithmetic mean of the Mathematics grades in Grade 10, Grade 11, and Grade 12, must be at least 7.00;
- The total average score, including additional points must be at least 7.00;
- Satisfy English requirements.
- Applicants must obtain a minimum total score of 15.00 out of 30 for three subjects (including compulsory Mathematics and Literature) in the High School
Graduation Examination (or the Vocational High School Examination for applicants9
from Vocational Education). This provision shall not apply to applicants who are
granted special consideration for high school graduation.

The Examination Committee will decide the admission score according to the
applicants' ranking, based on the total average score and admission quota. If the number
of qualified applicants is higher than the admission quota and the last ranked applicants
have equal scores, those with higher entrance English scores will be given the priority
for admission.The equivalency conversion among different English test formats shall be implemented in accordance with VGU regulations.

**Mode 3 – Direct admission**

**Mode 3 – Direct admission**

**1\. Eligible applicants:**

Applicants having excellent academic achievements could apply for a direct admission during the application period.

2\. Admission procedures:

Applicants are required to complete the application form and submit the following mandatory documents (scanned copy or photo) through VGU's online application system:

\- Personal identity card/passport;

\- Valid certificate or evidence of the academic achievement;

\- Valid English certificate (if any).

**3\. Requirements for selection:**

**Eligible applicants must belong to one of the types below:**

a) Being a member of the National team in the competition "International Olympiad" for the high school, following by one of these subjects: Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry, Informatics or winning the 1st, 2nd, 3rd prize in the high-school competition "Asian – Pacific Olympiad" in the subjects of Mathematics, Physics or Informatics.

b) Winning the 1st, 2nd, 3rd or Consolation prize in the Vietnam National High school competition or in the Provincial High school competition in subjects relevant to applied study programs:

    -  For CSE, ECE, MEN, SME, SPE, BCE, ARC, MEC and BME: accepted with the subjects of Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry and Informatics;

    -  For BFA, BBA, BSE, BDE: accepted with the subjects of Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry, Informatics, English, German, French and Literature.

c) Being a member of the National team in the International Science and Engineering Fair (ISEF) or winning the 1st, 2nd, 3rd prize of the Vietnam National Competition of Science and Technology. The result of the winning project must be consistent with the applied study program at VGU.

Satisfy English requirements.

The validation of the international, national and provincial prizes mentioned in Points a, b, c of this Clause are three (03) years from the time of winning.

**Applicants must satisfy the following requirements to be eligible for the selection:**

a) Satisfy the English requirements as stated in Article 5 of this Regulation. If applicants must take VGU ETEST, the test fee is 1,000,000 VND per applicant per test;

b) Applicants must obtain a minimum total score of 15.00 out of 30 for three subjects (including compulsory Mathematics and Literature) in the High School Graduation Examination (or the Vocational High School Examination for applicants from Vocational Education), applicable to candidates graduating from 2026 onwards.

This provision shall not apply to applicants who are granted special consideration for high school graduation, and to those who have won first, second, or third prizes in national or international competitions for excellent students, or in national or international science and technology contests organized or nominated for the applicant's participation by the Ministry of Education and Training.

**Mode 4 – International high school diplomas/certificates**

**Mode 4 – International high school diplomas/certificates**

**1\. Admission procedures:**

Applicants are required to complete the application form and submit the following mandatory documents (scanned copy or photo) through VGU's online application system:

\- Personal identity card/ passport;

\- High school academic records (Grades 10, 11 and 12);

\- Recognized international high school diplomas (IBD, AS/A-Level/IGCSE, WACE …) or certificates of international aptitude tests (SAT, TestAS);

\- Valid English certificate (if any).

\\* The original documents in Vietnamese or English language are accepted, otherwise certified translated copies are required.

2\. Requirements for selection:

Satisfy the English requirements as stated in Article 5 of this Regulation. If applicants must take VGU ETEST, the test fee is 1,000,000 VND per applicant per test;

Applicants must obtain a minimum total score of 15.00 out of 30 for three examination subjects (including compulsory Mathematics and Literature) in the High School Graduation Examination (or the Vocational High School Examination for applicants from Vocational Education), applicable to candidates graduating from 2026 onwards.

This provision shall not apply to applicants who are granted special consideration for high school graduation, and to those who have graduated from international high school programs.

**Mode 5 – High School Graduation Exam**

**Mode 5 – High School Graduation Exam**

**1\. Eligible applicants:**

Selection based on the applicants' results in the High School Graduation Exam of Vietnam.

**2\. Admission procedures:**

Applicants are required to complete the application form and submit the following mandatory documents (scanned copy or photo) through VGU's online application system (https://apply.vgu.edu.vn/en):

\- Personal identity card/ passport;

\- The official diploma or temporary certificate of High school graduation;

\- The result of High school Graduation Exam of the applied admission subject groups announced by the relevant authorities;

\- Authorized official high school academic records in three grades (Grades 10, 11 and 12)verified by the relevant high school;

\- Proof documents of priority type and priority area (if any);

\- Valid English certificate (if any).

If applicants must take VGU English test, the test fee is 1,000,000 VND per test.

3\. Requirements for admission:

\- The total score of three subjects of the applied admission block according to 30 grading scale (without multiplication factors), including the bonus points stipulated by the MoET must be equal to or higher than the VGU admission score for the respective admission block and program.

\- Satisfy English requirements. Score of a valid international English certificate (IELTS Academic, TOEFL iBT…) can be converted to an equivalent English score in the relevant admission blocks according to the English proficiency tests equivalency table here [[linked annex]](https://vgu.edu.vn/documents/14921/11688573/Annex+1.+English+proficiency+tests+equivalency+table.pdf/b9230ada-106c-457b-b2b1-e5a6c6b17536).

**English proficiency requirement:**

1\. Applicants are required to satisfy one of the following requirements for entrance English proficiency:

_-_ Have a valid international English certificate, equivalent to at least IELTS Academic 5.0 at the time of application. The equivalency of different English proficiency tests is detailed here _[[linked annex]](https://vgu.edu.vn/documents/14921/11688573/Annex+1.+English+proficiency+tests+equivalency+table.pdf/b9230ada-106c-457b-b2b1-e5a6c6b17536)_ ; or

\- Pass VGU English test which covers listening, speaking, reading and writing skills at least 75/100; or

\- Achieve at least 8.0 points (grading scale of 10 points) of the average English score in three grades of High school. For the BCE program specifically, the average English grade over the three years of upper secondary education must be at least 7.5 This requirement is only applicable for Mode 2 and Mode 5;

2\. Applicants are exempted from the English proficiency requirement if:

\- Applicants from one of the countries where English is an official language according to the list provided here [[linked annex]](https://vgu.edu.vn/documents/14921/10674296/List+of+countries+where+English+is+an+official+language.pdf/79e14290-d5be-4c35-a765-c338d40fad87).

\- Graduates from international high school programs where the language of instruction and examination is English. The list of recognized High school diplomas/certificates by country provided here [[linked annex]](https://vgu.edu.vn/documents/14921/10674296/List+of+recognized+Highschool+diplomas_certificates+by+country.pdf/479f96ca-48b7-4756-9cbf-2e0b16c0dba8) They must submit a letter of confirmation issued by their school, stating that the instruction and examination were conducted in English

\- The VGU's English test covers listening, speaking, reading, writing skills. For more information about the Sample tests please click here. [[linked annex]](https://vgu.edu.vn/en/admission/bachelor/sample-test)
