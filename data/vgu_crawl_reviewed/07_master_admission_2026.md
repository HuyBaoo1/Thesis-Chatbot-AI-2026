**4\. Admission requirements**

Applicants must meet requirements for the entrance English proficiency and the total score of Entrance Examination.

**4.1. Requirement for entrance** **English proficiency**

Applicants must satisfy one of the following requirements:

\- Have a valid international English certificate, equivalent to at least IELTS Academic 5.0 at the time of application. For GFE, applicants need an IELTS Academic certificate of 5.5 or equivalent. The equivalency of different English proficiency tests is detailed in Annex 1 of VGU's Admission Regulation for master's degree programs. If applicants have an IELTS certificate that expires during the application period and previously graduated from a bachelor or master program which fully taught and assessed in English, the expired IELTS certificate can be accepted for admission; or

\- Pass VGU English test; or

\- Applicants come from a country where English is an official language according to VGU's Admission Regulation for master's degree programs (Annex 2); or

\- Graduated from a bachelor or master program fully taught and assessed in English. Applicants are required to submit a letter of confirmation issued by their higher education, stating that the instruction and examination were fully conducted in English.

**4.2.** **The total score of Entrance Examination**

\- The total score of the Entrance Examination, on a scale of 10 and rounded to two decimal places using the half-up rounding method, are calculated using the following formula:

**The total score of the Entrance Examination = (25% x Bachelor GPA) + (50% x score for Technical Interview/written test) + (25% x score for VGU** **English** **test)**

\- Applicants are required to achieve the score for the Technical Interview/written test of at least 5.0. For GFE's candidates, a written exam is conducted for who do not submit specific certificates such as GMAT/GRE and an essay submission.

\- The English score for certificates such as IELTS Academic, TOEFL iBT and GPA will be converted to a 10-point scale according to VGU's Admission Regulation for master's degree programs.

\- Applicants are ranked from the highest to lowest based on the total score of the Entrance Examination and admitted according to the admission quotas for each program.

\- GFE is conducted under its own regulations and in compliance with the admission requirements of Goethe University Frankfurt, the partner institution.

**Please note that:**

\- The conditional admission letter is offered to applicants who do not yet have IELTS Academic 6.0 or TOEFL iBT 60 or equivalent and those who have not submitted their bachelor's degree yet.

\- VGU will organize Pre-Master Course(s) to enhance the English skills for those who cannot submit a proof of English language proficiency with a minimum band score of IELTS Academic 6.0 or TOEFL iBT 60 at the time of admission. Participants are responsible for paying the course fee regulated by VGU. Upon the completion of the Pre-Master Course(s), the participants are refunded 100% and 50% of the course fee if they submit IELTS Academic certificate with band score 7.0 and 6.5-6.0 respectively within 4 weeks. Further details on the Pre-Master Course(s) will be sent to the applicants upon the availability of their admission letters.

**6\. Application procedures**

Applicants are required to complete the application form and submit the following mandatory documents (scanned copy) through the online application system [https://apply.vgu.edu.vn](https://apply.vgu.edu.vn/) by the deadline as specified in session 3 of this announcement.

\- Personal identity card or passport;

\- Bachelor's degree or temporary statement of academic completion (if bachelor's degree is not yet available);

\- Bachelor's academic transcript;

\- Valid English certificate (if any);

\- Curriculum vitae (required for GPE and MBA);

\- For MBA and certain applicants for BIS and GPE: documents demonstrating relevant working experience.

\- For GFE only: letter of motivation, letter of reference/evaluation, evidence of mathematical/quantitative knowledge (score GRE/GMAT or equivalent), essay in English (limited to 2000 words).

_Documents have to be presented in Vietnamese or English language, otherwise certified translation is required._
