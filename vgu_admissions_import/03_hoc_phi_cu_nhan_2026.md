## International-student exception

Students from lower-middle-income and low-income countries, as well as students from German exchange partner universities, are entitled to pay tuition fees at the same rates as Vietnamese students.

Students from countries not covered by the exchange/bilateral/low-income exceptions pay tuition fees equal to 1.5 times the regular tuition fees applicable to Vietnamese students.

## Fees not included in regular tuition

The tuition fee does not cover non-regular academic fees or student-service fees, including:

- health insurance;
- mandatory additional course fees;
- administrative fees;
- teaching fee at partner university;
- entrance examination fees;
- dormitory accommodation;
- bus services.

These fees are governed separately or may be adjusted annually according to actual circumstances and applicable regulations.

## Payment deadline and method

Regular tuition fee or student status fee is collected per semester. Tuition fee must be paid in full before:

- Summer semester: before 28 February.
- Winter semester: before 31 August.

VGU accepts various tuition and fee payment methods, including credit card or bank transfer to the university bank account.

## Source notes

- Decision No. 239/QD-DHVD comes into force from the signing date, 25 March 2026.
- The decision replaces Decision No. 74/QD-DHVD dated 19 February 2025 and Decision No. 186/QD-DHVD dated 24 April 2025.
- Tuition fees remain applicable throughout the regular duration of study for a given student intake. If students are transferred from an earlier intake to a new intake, the tuition fees applicable to the new intake apply.
